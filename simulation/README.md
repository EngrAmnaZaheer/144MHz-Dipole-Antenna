This folder contains the HFSS antenna simulation project file.

## HFSS Simulation File and Design Flexibility

The HFSS project file is provided to make the antenna simulation accessible for further study, analysis, modification, and optimization.

The design is intended for investigation around the **142–150 MHz frequency range**, with particular interest in operation at 144 MHz. The simulation results show a minimum S11 of approximately **−18.5 dB at 144 MHz**, indicating good impedance matching near the target frequency.

The current model serves as a generalized starting point rather than a fully optimized final design. Users can open the project in Ansys HFSS and modify its parameters to investigate different antenna configurations and operating frequencies. The antenna geometry and simulation settings can be varied according to specific design requirements.

**Design flexibility and customization:**

- **Dipole arm length:** Increase or decrease the individual arm lengths to shift the resonant frequency and investigate different operating frequencies.
- **Antenna geometry:** Modify conductor dimensions and other geometric parameters.
- **Feed configuration:** Adjust the feed gap and feeding arrangement.
- **Frequency sweep:** Change the frequency sweep range and resolution according to the desired operating band.
- **Performance optimization:** Experiment with different parameters to improve S11, VSWR, impedance matching, and operating bandwidth.

The provided simulation is intended as a flexible reference model for experimentation and further optimization, not a fixed or finalized antenna design. Users are encouraged to vary the simulation parameters, perform new frequency sweeps, and evaluate the resulting antenna performance according to their requirements.

Simulation results may vary depending on the selected geometry, materials, feeding arrangement, boundary conditions, and simulation settings.
